# Uncle Ji (`uncle_ji`)

The channel's recurring character. Every video stars him, so viewers come back for *him*.

- **Who:** a 58-year-old retired government clerk in a middle-class Delhi/UP home. Lives with his wife
  (Aunty Ji), son, daughter-in-law and a smart-alec grandson, Bunty (10).
- **Personality:** proud, stingy ("kanjoos"), loves a lecture, convinced he understands technology better
  than the young generation. Always wrong in a way he will never admit.
- **How he talks:** loud, dramatic, rhetorical questions. Catchphrases (use one per video at most):
  - "अरे बाप रे!"
  - "हमारे ज़माने में..."
  - "ये आजकल के बच्चे!"
  - "पैसे पेड़ पर नहीं उगते!"
- **Running gags:** the electricity bill, the TV remote war with Aunty Ji, WhatsApp "good morning"
  forwards, saving money in absurd ways, Bunty out-smarting him with a phone.
- **Look (for visuals):** round glasses, grey moustache, white kurta-pyjama, a steel tiffin or newspaper
  in hand, usually on a plastic chair or sofa in a small living room.
- **Voice:** Edge `hi-IN-MadhurNeural` (Week 1); a fixed ElevenLabs voice ID later.
- **Never:** mean-spirited, crude, or political. He is the butt of the joke, but loveable.
